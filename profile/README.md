## From the first prompt to a product people rely on

Fluxzero is the all-in-one European cloud with frontend, backend, data, security, and observability working as one. You and your coding agent build the product behavior. Fluxzero runs the cloud your product needs.

- **Spend prompts on your product.** Focus on features, workflows, and user experience with fewer repeated backend decisions.
- **Build with peace of mind.** Reliability, permissions, recovery, and visibility are built into the cloud from day one.
- **Keep building as you grow.** Start locally for free, launch on Fluxzero Cloud, and scale without rebuilding the application architecture.

Your application is real code that you own, inspect, test, and extend.

## Start building

1. **[Open the getting-started guide](https://fluxzero.io/get-started).** Bring your idea and your coding agent.
2. **[Connect your agent](https://github.com/fluxzero-io/fluxzero-agent-plugins).** Plugins are available for Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot, with an equivalent Gemini CLI extension.
3. **Build locally, then launch.** The [Fluxzero CLI](https://github.com/fluxzero-io/fluxzero-cli) and [Dev Server](https://github.com/fluxzero-io/fluxzero-dev-server) support the development loop; [Fluxzero Cloud](https://fluxzero.io) runs the product in production.
